# Trademarks and the logo

The name "knobs" and the knob logo are trademarks of Daniel Alyoshin. This page says how you can use them, and why the logo files aren't under the GPL like the rest of knobs.

## The logo isn't under the GPL

knobs's code is licensed under GPL-2.0-or-later ([LICENSE](LICENSE)). The files in [`assets/`](assets/) aren't: the knob mark, the app icon and the lockups with the wordmark. They're Copyright © 2026 Daniel Alyoshin, all rights reserved, apart from what this page allows. Official builds of knobs carry the app icon, `assets/knobs.ico`.

## What you can do without asking

- Share unmodified copies of the knobs source, `assets/` included, and of the official builds from this repository's [releases](https://github.com/danielalyoshin/knobs/releases).
- Use the name and the logo, unmodified, to refer to knobs: in articles, videos, tutorials, reviews, package listings and links to the project.
- Say in plain text that your project works with knobs or is based on it.

## What needs permission

- **Releasing a modified knobs under its name or logo.** A fork that you distribute needs its own name and its own icon. Replace the files in `assets/`, `knobs.ico` included, since the build puts it in the exe, and change the name in `src/app_info.h`.
- **Changing the logo**, such as recoloring, rotating or stretching the knob, or using it as part of another logo.
- **Suggesting that knobs endorses you**, your product or your fork.

To ask, [open an issue](https://github.com/danielalyoshin/knobs/issues).
